Privacy Policy

Trading Alerts Mail Sync · Effective September 29, 2026

Who uses this app

Trading Alerts Mail Sync is a personal tool with a single user, its owner. It is not offered to or used by anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

The app requests read-only access to Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) for the owner's own account. It reads trading alert emails from Benzinga and VitalKnowledge in the owner's mailbox. It cannot send, delete or change email.

How the data is used

Alert emails are parsed into a database on the owner's computer (for example tickers, headlines, analyst actions and earnings figures) and used only for the owner's own market research.

Sharing

  • The data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.
  • It is processed on the owner's computer and by tools the owner uses at the owner's direction, such as AI assistants that query the local database.
  • It is not shared with anyone else.

Storage and retention

The imported data and the Google access token are stored on the owner's computer. The owner decides how long to keep them and can delete them at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date changed.
